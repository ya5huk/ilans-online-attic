This picture wasn't intended to receive the amount of value it has. On October 7th, 2023 my family and I were in London. We woke up to news which just didn't sound real. Our flight home was scheduled to the night of October 7th and was cancelled on the morning of that day. Seeing the news, everyone just wanted to get back home. We live in the south and so our family and friends were in real danger. We returned home on October 9th and hundreds of days full of extremely hard work and rebuild began.
