A tool that upon entering couple of details, generates a custom calendar that fits your needs and availability. The calendar can be exported to your calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, etc.), PDF or you can receive a daily newsletter with your daily tasks and more. The link is Vercel's because I stopped paying for the calendarplusai.com domain. This project is archived from the publishing perspective.
