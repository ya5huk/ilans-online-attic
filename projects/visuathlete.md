My first and biggest project thus far. A website made to visualize and analyse all of the results of Israel's athletes, retired and active. The website stores data of more than 1400 athletes about their performances. There's a page for each signed user with beautiful graphs and statistics. It gets updated as they compete. There is a home page feed, a leaderboard, articles, instagram profile for the site and much more. Gets ~400 users monthly.
